Dallas Popcorn Ceiling Removal Specialists

One trade, done properly: popcorn ceiling removal is all we do, and that focus is exactly why Dallas homeowners call us instead of a general painter.

Why a popcorn ceiling specialist — not a general painter

Most Dallas painters list popcorn ceiling removal as one line among dozens of services. We do one thing: popcorn, acoustic, and textured ceiling removal across Dallas–Fort Worth. That focus shows up in the details generalists skip — asbestos-safe work practices on pre-1980s texture, true dust containment, and skim coating finished to a Level 4 or Level 5 smooth surface instead of a quick scrape-and-paint.

What we do

  • Popcorn ceiling removal for bedrooms, living rooms, hallways, and whole homes
  • Acoustic and stipple textured ceiling removal
  • Asbestos testing coordination before any pre-1980s ceiling is disturbed
  • Drywall repair and skim coating after texture removal
  • Smooth, knockdown, and orange-peel ceiling finishes, plus priming and painting
  • Drywall overlay (encapsulation) where removal isn't the right answer

How we work

Every project follows the same controlled sequence: inspect the existing texture and paint history, seal the room in protective sheeting, remove the texture with the method matched to the coating, repair and skim the drywall, then prime and finish. Floors, fixtures, and HVAC vents stay protected throughout, and the room is HEPA-cleaned before we leave.
